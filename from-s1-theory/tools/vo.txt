Is Fromville a supernatural trap... or an experiment someone is running?
Season 1 gives us the kind of clues that fuel the experiment theory.
The town has electricity, but the wires don't connect to a normal power source.
Then Jim gets a voice on the radio that knows Tabitha is digging beneath the house.
The voice sounds less like a random signal, and more like someone watching them.
And when the radio tower goes up, a storm destroys it. That could mean the town reacts whenever people get too close to escaping... or learning how it works.
Season 1 never confirms there's a lab, or a human experiment. The supernatural explanation still fits.
But if someone is observing the town, the radio warning means they can see what the residents do. Who do you think is on the other end?
