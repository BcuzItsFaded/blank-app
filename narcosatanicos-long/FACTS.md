# The Narcosatanists (Matamoros, 1989) — factual notes
Episode 27 of **PLOT TWIST WITH A&R** (paper-cut lightbox, ~61s, English). Condensed from the
three-case script and fact-checked against Wikipedia ("Adolfo Constanzo", "Murder of Mark Kilroy").

## Documented
- **Mark Kilroy**, 21, a University of Texas pre-med student, disappeared in **Matamoros** during
  spring break in **March 1989** (sources give 13 or 14 March).
- On **1 April 1989**, **Serafín Hernández García** ran a drug checkpoint. Police followed him to
  **Rancho Santa Elena**.
- **Fifteen bodies** were dug up at the ranch, including Kilroy's. A ritual cauldron (nganga) was found.
- The leader was **Adolfo de Jesús Constanzo** ("El Padrino"), born 1962 in Miami. He told
  followers that ritual killings would protect them.
- On **6 May 1989**, cornered in **Mexico City**, he ordered a follower to shoot him.

## Corrected from the source script
- "27 bodies": the sources say 15 at the ranch, so the film says fifteen.
- "Believed he was invisible": this comes from popular accounts and is not in Wikipedia. The film
  says "according to later accounts", and the card reads "INVISIBLE"?
- "Held ~12 hours", "62 years for Sara Aldrete": the sources conflict or don't support these, so
  both were omitted.

## Long version (~2:25): added detail
- Kilroy, 21, crossed into Matamoros on the night of 13 March 1989. About 15,000 spring-break visitors were in
  town. He was last seen around 2 a.m. near a bar.
- The search: friends searched until morning. His parents circulated 20,000+ flyers and offered a $15,000 reward.
  The case aired on *America's Most Wanted* on 26 March.
- 1 April: Serafín Hernández García ran a federal drug checkpoint, and federal police followed him to Rancho Santa
  Elena, where they found cult objects and drug traces. On 9 April they arrested Serafín, his uncle Elio Hernández,
  and three others (David Serna Valdez, Sergio Martínez Salinas, Domingo Reyes Bustamante). Serafín said killings
  there had been ordered by Constanzo.
- 15 bodies were recovered, all male, most of them rival drug dealers. Kilroy was identified by dental records.
- Constanzo: born 1 Nov 1962 in Miami. His mother practised Palo Mayombe. In Mexico City he read tarot for
  wealthy clients, reportedly including traffickers and corrupt police. He struck a protection deal with the
  Hernández family.
- Sara Aldrete, "La Madrina": an honors student at Texas Southmost College, Constanzo's second-in-command.
  She was arrested on 6 May 1989 at the scene in Mexico City and sentenced in 1994 to more than 60 years. She
  maintains her innocence.
- Constanzo's death on 6 May 1989: he ordered Álvaro de León ("El Duby") to shoot him. Sources call it both
  an "assisted suicide" and an ordered killing.
- The Kilroy family started the Mark Kilroy Foundation (drug-education focus, per later reporting).
