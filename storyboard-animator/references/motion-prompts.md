# Motion prompt examples

Use these as patterns, not templates. Good `video_prompt`s describe one clear camera move, what physically moves in the frame, where subjects look, the pacing and the mood. They are at least 60 words long and never include talking.

## Photo of a person (portrait)
Slow, steady push-in from a medium shot toward the subject's face over the full scene. The subject blinks naturally, their gaze drifts from slightly off-camera to the lens, and a soft smile forms. Loose strands of hair move gently in a light breeze. Background stays softly out of focus with subtle light shimmer. Calm, warm, intimate pacing. Mouth stays closed — no speaking.

## Landscape / travel photo
Smooth lateral pan from left to right across the scene at a gentle, constant speed. Clouds drift slowly across the sky, water ripples and catches the light, and tree leaves sway lightly. A few birds cross far in the distance. Golden-hour lighting holds steady. Peaceful, cinematic pacing with no sudden movement and no camera shake.

## Product shot
Slow orbit of about 20 degrees around the product, keeping it centered and sharp. Soft studio light slides across its surface, producing a moving highlight that reveals texture and edges. The background stays clean and still. Subtle depth-of-field shift brings the logo into crisp focus by the end. Premium, deliberate, unhurried pacing.

## Illustrated / cartoon character (storyboard mode)
Static wide shot. The character (same outfit and colors as the reference sheet) walks from frame left to center, stops, and looks up in surprise at something above frame. Their ears perk up and eyes widen. Background elements — swaying grass, a drifting cloud — keep gentle motion. Playful, bouncy timing. The character does not talk.
