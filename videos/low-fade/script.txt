Most messy fades come from one mistake: going too high, too fast. Here's a clean low fade in five steps. Step one. Set your guide. Clippers open, no guard. Cut a line about an inch above the ear, all the way around the back. Step two. Number one guard. Start below the line and flick out just above it with a scooping motion. Step three. Number two guard. Go a little higher to soften the jump from the one. Step four. Hunt the lines. Close the lever and blend every hard edge in small strokes. Check the mirror from every angle. Step five. Clean it up. Trimmer on the hairline and around the ears, then connect the top with scissors over comb. Low, tight, and blended. Which step messes you up the most? Tell me in the comments.
