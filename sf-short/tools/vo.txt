In 1987, Ryu wins the first World Warrior tournament. Then Akuma kills his master, Gouken, and Ryu disappears.
Six years later, Chun-Li, secretly working for Interpol, recruits Ryu, and his estranged friend Ken, for a new tournament.
The host is M. Bison, and the whole tournament is a trap to steal Ryu's power.
Ryu gets captured and forfeits. Ken fights his way to the final, beating Balrog and Dhalsim.
But Ken walks away from the final to break Ryu out, handing the title to a fighter named Joe.
Then Akuma arrives. Ryu's dark power takes over, and he beats Akuma as Evil Ryu.
Ken brings his friend back with a Hadouken of his own. But Bison escapes, taking Akuma with him.
And Bison finds a new ally: Sagat. Round two is coming.
