# THE TOMB IN HAVANA (LA MILAGROSA) — treatment
PLOT TWIST WITH A&R · Episode 22 · vertical 9:16 · 30.5s · paper-cut lightbox · English

**Look.** Colón Cemetery's white marble glowing under a cold moon: angel statues, the domed
central chapel and royal palms. The single warm accent is the red roses at the end, plus
the small brass glints of the tomb's iron rings. A small A&R badge sits in the corner throughout.

1. **0:00 Colón Cemetery.** Angels, the chapel, palms. Cards: THE TOMB IN HAVANA / LA MILAGROSA.
2. **0:05 He knocks.** Her husband lifts the iron ring and knocks three times, then backs away
   without turning. Card: AMELIA GOYRI · 1901.
3. **0:10 The legend turns.** Close on the sealed slab (AMELIA GOYRI DE ADOT · † 1901) as the moon
   slips behind cloud.
4. **0:13 At her feet.** A cut-away beneath the slab shows her coffin, then a tiny coffin at her feet.
5. **0:16 Opened years later.** The slab grinds back and light pours in. A glowing, shrouded
   figure lies UNTOUCHED, with THE BABY IN HER ARMS.
6. **0:24 La Milagrosa.** Her marble statue with cross and child, brass rings and red roses.
   Cards: LOVE BEYOND DEATH? / OR A STORY WE COULDN'T LET GO? Then silence: PLOT TWIST WITH A&R.

**Sound.** A low storyteller voice over distant wind and a far church bell. Three iron-ring
knocks on marble, footsteps backing away, the stone slab grinding open. A mother's lullaby on
glockenspiel and harp over a low cello, with a soft string-and-bowed-vibraphone swell at the
reveal. Then [Silence.]: the music cuts away, and one final knock comes from the tomb.
