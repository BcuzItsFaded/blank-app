# The Tomb in Havana (La Milagrosa) — factual notes

Episode 22 of **PLOT TWIST WITH A&R** (paper-cut lightbox, 30s, English).
**A real person and a real tradition. The supernatural events are folklore, not verified facts.**

## Documented / widely reported
- **Amelia Goyri de la Hoz** (Amelia Goyri de Adot) is buried in Havana's **Colón Cemetery**
  (Necrópolis de Cristóbal Colón). She died in **1901**, in childbirth. Her baby daughter
  died too and was buried with her, at her feet according to the accounts.
- Her husband, **José Vicente Adot**, visited her tomb daily for years. He knocked with one
  of the tomb's iron rings as if to wake her, and left walking backwards so as never to
  turn his back on her.
- Her tomb carries a white marble **statue of a woman holding a cross and a baby**
  (by sculptor José Vilalta de Saavedra). Known as **La Milagrosa** ("the miraculous one"),
  it is one of the most visited graves in Cuba. Pilgrims knock with the ring, leave flowers,
  ask for favours (often about pregnancy and children's health) and walk away backwards.

## Legend (not verified)
- That when the tomb was opened years later (often said to be 1914), Amelia's body was
  **incorrupt** and the baby, buried at her feet, was **found in her arms**. This is the
  heart of the popular legend. No verified record confirms it. The film presents it with
  "they say" and closes on the script's own question.

## Depiction
- The coffins are shown only as lit outlines in a cut-away. The "body" in the reveal is a
  glowing, shrouded figure with no visible face.
- The year on the slab shows only † 1901. Her birth year was not verified for this film.
