# La Llorona (paper-cut lightbox): treatment

Style: Paper-cut Lightbox. 9:16 vertical, 30 fps, 60.4 s, English narration, serif subtitles on the dark front sheet; scene labels as glowing cut-paper strips.

**Structure chosen:** eight backlit dioramas, each a shallow box of layered cut paper lit from behind, carrying the legend from its oldest root to its final warning. The weeping woman is the single pale, translucent, *glowing* sheet among dark silhouettes — a ghost literally made of light — because the story is about a woman of water and night who is seen but never grasped.

**Why this style:** water and night are the legend's two constants, and the lightbox renders both as transmitted light: a river is a bright sheet with ripple cuts, the moon a warm disc reflected in wavy streaks, and La Llorona herself the one luminous figure. Her cry is drawn as expanding pale arcs over the water.

**Arc:** a night river with reeds and a distant weeping woman (the hook) → an ancient stepped temple, a woman crying in the dark (the oldest warning) → a pre-conquest city with a canal, she wanders crying out → Cihuacóatl, a jade-lit temple, glyph and goddess (the forgotten goddess) → a colonial town, María, the man leaving, the children slipping into the river (the tragic legend) → her spirit over a wide moonlit river, crying "¡Ay, mis hijos!" (the eternal punishment) → a map of Mexico with many rivers and several pale women (the real mystery, different regions) → a dark river at night, the warning "Never follow the crying near the water," the woman dissolving into mist.
**Colour:** cool indigo/teal water and night throughout, a warm amber moon, and one jade accent for the goddess; the woman's pale glow is the recurring light.
**Native moves:** light-through-paper (the glowing ghost); the lamp/moon reflected on water; depth as distance (parallax layers, cast shadows); the cry as ripples of light.
**Sound:** a music-box lullaby (for the mother) over cellos and harp, turning sorrowful; a continuous river bed with drips and wind; a distant woman's wail on each cry; two splashes and a silence at the drowning; a hush before the final warning.
**Rendering note:** a Canvas 2D emulation of the lightbox (sheets with fibre grain, a warm rim and dark band, composited back to front with backlight glow, bloom and cast shadows), since the container has no GPU.
