# La Planchada (scratchboard long take): treatment

Style: Scratchboard Long Take. 9:16 vertical, 30 fps, 62.4 s. English narration; subtitles on an ink band. Ink + warm paper + one red, with a warm yellow reserved for light.

**Structure chosen:** one unbroken camera move travelling left→right through a long printed hospital — exterior, corridor, garden memory, rain, ward, morning lobby, a portrait, and back to the exterior — the eight narration beats placed along the move, the camera passing through each section's centre at the beat's midpoint and never cutting. Considered: a sequence of locked plates (that is woodcut, not this style) and a vertical rise (loses the horizontal layering). Chosen because the long take IS the style: depth, weather and light are all knife cuts, and the camera travels through the print rather than turning pages.

**The one red:** reserved for La Planchada herself — the small red cross on her cap (her trace through every scene), the red heart of her love (which splits at the betrayal and rises again at the end), and the single lit red window where she is. Never a second accent. The sun and a lamp carry the one allowed warm yellow.

**Arc:** a high-wide of the hospital at night, one red window, the sky carved in horizontal cuts → a low glide down the corridor, her pressed uniform the only paper figure → through the steam into a warm memory, the doctor walking off, her heart splitting → through the steam to a grey rain-page, her spirit pulled back by a thread → the ward, she tends a bed, a red lamp → the sky carved open to day in the morning lobby, staff who do not know her, question marks → a held portrait of her with a mourning ribbon, a patient who runs → through the steam to the final exterior, the red heart rising among the windows, the camera pulling back to the whole hospital.
**Native moves:** tone is cut not shaded (every surface an ink field with paper cuts, or paper with ink cuts); the sky carved open (night stars → dawn → dense day cuts); through the steam (paper-cut whiteout thresholds); one red waking (the heart, the window).
**Benchmark (grammar only):** wood engraving / scratchboard cut-line tone, shin-hanga layered planes, and the unbroken tracking shot of nature documentaries. No existing print, figure or film route is copied.
**Sound:** a small chamber group (bowed vibraphone theme, harp, horn, low strings, pizzicato bass, xylophone, glockenspiel, one timpani stroke); the print sounds (knife through paper, a sheet torn away) are the loudest events and fall only on the steam thresholds; silence before the verdict at 46.7–49.1 s, then one timpani stroke. Music ducks far under the narration.
**Rendering note:** a Canvas 2D emulation of the scratchboard (ink/paper cut-line fields, parallax strips sliding like a printed diorama, a print pass of paper fibre, flicker and vignette) because the container has no GPU for the library's three.js path.
