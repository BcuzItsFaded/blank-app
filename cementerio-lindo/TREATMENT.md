# CEMENTERIO LINDO: THE FORGOTTEN DEAD — treatment
PLOT TWIST WITH A&R · Episode 21 · vertical 9:16 · ~60s · paper-cut lightbox · English

**Look.** Cold moonlit cut paper in silver-blue, with drifting dust, low mist and deep
shadows. Phoenix appears as Camelback Mountain and saguaro silhouettes on the horizon.
Endless rows of simple crosses and worn markers recede into the dark. The only warm
colour is the marigolds and candles of the living remembering, saved for the end.
A small A&R badge sits in the corner throughout.

1. **0:00 Hook.** Thousands of graves under the moon. Cards: CEMENTERIO LINDO / THE FORGOTTEN DEAD.
2. **0:05 Phoenix.** A wrought-iron gate reading CEMENTERIO LINDO, saguaros, and the city's
   lights beyond Camelback. Then ESTABLISHED 1890 / THOUSANDS BURIED · MANY TOO POOR.
3. **0:17 Names lost.** The names fade off the markers while a woman with a lantern searches the rows.
4. **0:23 After sunset.** A walk down the gravel path. Footsteps behind you. A whip-turn: NOBODY'S THERE.
5. **0:30 Remember me.** Pale spirits of light rise from the graves while forgotten names drift upward.
6. **0:40 Don't forget us again.** You run, the gate swings open, and the spirits follow.
7. **0:43 The truth.** The spirits vanish. What remains is sunken, unmarked plots labelled UNKNOWN.
   Cards: NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE / BUT THE FORGOTTEN GRAVES ARE REAL.
8. **0:50 Remembered.** Marigolds bloom on grave after grave, candles are lit, and the names glow
   back onto the markers. Cards: NEARLY ERASED FROM HISTORY / REMEMBER THEM / PLOT TWIST WITH A&R.

**Sound.** British storyteller narration. The ghost voices ("Remember me…", names, "Don't forget
us again") are spoken lines turned into breathy whispers with a 16-band noise vocoder, then
reverbed and spread around you. Desert crickets, a distant coyote, the gate creaking in the
wind, your steps on the gravel and a second set behind you slightly out of step. The score is
bowed vibraphone and glass harp over low strings. For the remembrance it turns warm: a nylon-string
guitar elegy in A minor and a distant bell. Mastered to −14 LUFS.
