# Cementerio Lindo — factual notes

Episode 21 of **PLOT TWIST WITH A&R** (paper-cut lightbox, ~60s, English).
**Real history + a fictionalized haunting.**

## Documented history
- **Cementerio Lindo** is a historic burial ground in Phoenix, Arizona. It was used by
  Maricopa County as a burial ground from **1890** to about 1951/52 and is believed to
  be the county's first cemetery. It was later deeded to the City of Phoenix and renamed
  Cementerio Lindo.
- It served largely **poor and predominantly minority residents**, including many
  Mexican-American families and immigrants, who helped build early Phoenix.
- **Thousands** are buried there. A 2008 Class III archaeological survey found records
  of more than 3,000 burials, and other estimates run higher (up to about 7,000). Many
  sources cite more than 5,000.
- **Lost markers:** the same survey recorded only about 632 surface markers. Neglect and
  vandalism took many headstones, and many burials have no known location today.
  Families and genealogists often can't find where a relative lies. The City of Phoenix
  and volunteers have worked to restore it.

## Fictionalized for this episode
- The footsteps, the whisper "Remember me", the voices calling names, and the spirits
  are **invented**. The narration says so plainly ("No verified evidence confirms these
  ghostly voices").
- The names on screen (María, José, Guadalupe, …) are common names chosen to suggest
  the people buried there. **They are not records of specific burials.**
- The marigolds (cempasúchil) and candles in the finale are a remembrance motif, not a
  depiction of a particular event.
