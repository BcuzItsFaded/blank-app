# SAN MIGUEL CANOA, 1968 — treatment
PLOT TWIST WITH A&R · Episode 23 · vertical 9:16 · ~61s · paper-cut lightbox · English

**Look.** Cut paper. A dusk climb under La Malinche, then a cold, rain-swept night in Canoa:
adobe houses, the church and its bell tower. The only warm light is menacing: the mob's
torches. The deaths are never shown. They are four candle flames going out, one by one.
A small A&R badge sits in the corner throughout.

1. **0:00 The climb.** Five hikers with packs walk a ridge under La Malinche at dusk. Cards:
   SAN MIGUEL CANOA / PUEBLA, MEXICO · 1968 / A DOCUMENTED TRUE STORY / THEY ONLY NEEDED A PLACE TO SLEEP.
2. **0:10 Rain.** They reach the village in heavy rain, and a doorway lights up: LUCAS GARCÍA OFFERED SHELTER.
3. **0:19 The sermon.** The priest at the pulpit, his shadow growing huge on the wall.
   FEAR WAS PREACHED / "THE COMMUNISTS ARE COMING".
4. **0:25 The bells.** The bell swings in its tower and the street fills with torches and machetes.
   THE CHURCH BELLS RANG / "¡YA LLEGARON LOS COMUNISTAS!"
5. **0:32 Surrounded.** A ring of torches around the lit house. NEARLY 800 PEOPLE.
6. **0:35 Four dead.** Four flames on the sill go out as the names appear: JESÚS CARRILLO
   SÁNCHEZ · RAMÓN GUTIÉRREZ CALVARIO · LUCAS GARCÍA · 4 DEAD · 3 GRAVELY INJURED.
7. **0:46 Dawn.** La Malinche in cold morning light, with a pack, a coil of rope and a boot
   left in the rain. NOT AGITATORS / HIKERS.
8. **0:50 Never convicted.** The church at night. The priest steps inside and the door closes on
   the light. THE PRIEST WAS NEVER CONVICTED / NO GHOST · ONLY A RUMOR / AND A CROWD THAT
   BELIEVED IT / PLOT TWIST WITH A&R.

**Sound.** Boots on the mountain path and a nylon guitar at dusk, then rain and thunder.
Alarm bells over a quickening timpani pulse. A crowd murmur swells into a roar, with
crackling torches and fists on the door. Then it stops dead: only rain, a thunderclap and a
snuffed flame for each life. A solo-cello lament in D minor plays over low strings, the
church door closes on a single bell, and the music fades to silence. Mastered to −14 LUFS.
