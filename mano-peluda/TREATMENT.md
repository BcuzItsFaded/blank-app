# LA MANO PELUDA — treatment

**PLOT TWIST WITH A&R · Episode 18**
Vertical 9:16 · ~60.6s · paper-cut lightbox · English narration (bilingual: keeps
the Spanish name & cards) · dark bedtime-horror.

## Logline
A severed hairy hand that crawls from under the bed is Mexico's most intimate
night terror. The film lays its two origin legends — buried gold, and the
Inquisition — against the rational explanation (sleep paralysis), and lands on the
one rule every version shares: keep your feet under the blanket.

## Look
Layered cut-paper silhouettes, warm backlight, cast shadows, film grain. Deep
indigo darkness; a single warm candle/lamp is the only light. The **hairy hand** is
the recurring motif — an ink silhouette with bristles — read against lit paper so
the dark shape always shows. One gold accent (the buried treasure) is the only
other colour note.

## Beats (narration → image)
1. **0.0–7.6 — The hook.** A dark bedroom, a sleeper, a candle. Something stirs
   under the bed. Title card **LA MANO PELUDA / THE HAIRY HAND**.
2. **7.6–16.1 — The hairy hand.** Close on the hand crawling across the warm-lit
   floor, bristles and splayed fingers.
3. **16.1–24.6 — The buried man.** A graveyard under an eclipsed moon; the hand
   claws up from an open grave over a chest of gold. Card **ORO**.
4. **24.6–31.9 — The tortured soul.** The Inquisition: stone arches, a brazier, a
   hooded figure, the hand severed and returning. Card **LA INQUISICIÓN**.
5. **31.9–39.3 — The night visit.** The hand creeps up the bedframe toward a foot
   left hanging out — and closes on the ankle. Hard musical cut.
6. **39.3–45.4 — The elders' warning.** A grandmother tells two children by candle;
   the music-box tune turns this into a bedtime rhyme. A hand droops off a bed
   behind them.
7. **45.4–52.6 — The real mystery.** Sleep paralysis: a frozen sleeper, a red-eyed
   weight crouched on the chest, faint reaching hands. Card **¿PARÁLISIS DEL SUEÑO?**
8. **52.6–60.6 — The final twist.** The sleeper pulls their feet under the blanket;
   the hand sinks away, foiled. Card **KEEP YOUR FEET UNDER THE BLANKET.** — and one
   last little scratch: *just in case.*

## Sound
British-storyteller narration (Kokoro/edge-tts). Score: a sinister A-minor
music-box over low cello/contrabass drones, tremolo dread, and a quickening
timpani heartbeat that spikes at the grip and cuts dead. Foley: under-bed
scratching that drags across the stereo field as the hand crawls, bedframe
creaks, a brazier crackle, the severing snap, a chest-weight thud and breaths in
the paralysis beat, and a blanket rustle at the end. Mastered to −14 LUFS.

Everything is drawn and synthesised in code — no photos, stock, or AI imagery.
