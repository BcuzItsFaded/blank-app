Street Fighter's ending brings back a fighter Ryu already beat once. And you only see him if you stay for the credits.

Spoilers from here.

1987. Ryu wins the first World Warrior tournament using the Dark Hadou. Akuma wants that power, so he kills Ryu's master, Gouken, to drag it back out.

Six years later, M. Bison hosts a second tournament. It's bait. He wants Ryu strapped into a machine that drains his power.

Then Ryu confesses. He tore up Ken's invitation to that first tournament. Pure jealousy. The brothers fight, and Ken loses.

Ken never shows up for the final, so Joe, played by UFC star Alexander Volkanovski, wins by forfeit. Akuma walks in and finishes him in seconds.

Ryu goes Dark Ryu. He beats Akuma, then turns on his own friends. Nobody can stop him until Ken throws a flaming Hadouken he's never pulled off before.

Then the twist hits. Bison's soldiers carry off Akuma's unconscious body, and Cammy blows up Chun-Li's van to cover the escape. Bison's new plan is to steal Akuma's power instead of Ryu's.

Now the mid-credits scene. It's animated. A man with an eyepatch and a huge scar across his chest is training. Bison walks up with Vega, Cammy and Juli.

That's Sagat, the fighter Ryu beat in 1987. In the games, he's Bison's bodyguard, and Ryu's Shoryuken gave him that scar.
