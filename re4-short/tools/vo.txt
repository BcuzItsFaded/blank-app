The President's daughter has been kidnapped. One agent is sent to bring her back: Leon Kennedy.
In rural Spain, the villagers aren't just hostile. They're infected with a parasite called Las Plagas.
Pitchforks, torches, and a man with a chainsaw. The whole village wants Leon dead.
Leon finds Ashley in a church, but the cult has already infected them both.
The cult, Los Illuminados, wants to send Ashley home, and use her parasite to control the President.
Through Salazar's castle, and a secret island lab, Leon fights his way to her, with help from the mysterious Ada Wong.
A machine in the lab burns out their parasites, and Ada tosses Leon a rocket launcher to finish Saddler.
Leon and Ashley escape as the island explodes. But Ada flies off with a Plaga sample... and the story is far from over.
