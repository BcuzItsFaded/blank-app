# Plot Twist with A&R — Episode 5: The Woman with Eight Names

Real case: the Isdal Woman (Isdalen, near Bergen, Norway, 29 November 1970). Unidentified to this day.

**Fact-check:** Discovery date and place, removed clothing labels, luggage at Bergen railway station
linked by fingerprints, wigs, foreign currency, a coded notebook (decoded as dates and places), multiple
hotel aliases, and the unresolved identity are documented. Sources differ on 8 vs 9 aliases; "at least
eight" stays accurate. The spy idea is speculation and is labelled as such.

Sources: [Wikipedia — Isdal Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isdal_Woman),
[Life in Norway](https://www.lifeinnorway.net/isdal-woman/),
[Discovery UK](https://www.discoveryuk.com/?p=19876540).
