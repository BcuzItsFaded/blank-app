The Green Mile's ending is darker than you remember. Spoilers!

John Coffey could heal people, by pulling their sickness into himself. When he took the guard Paul Edgecomb's hand, he passed a piece of that power into Paul.

So Paul never stopped living. In the final scene, he's one hundred and eight years old, and he's watched everyone he loves die.

He believes it's his punishment, for executing an innocent miracle. And the mouse, Mr. Jingles? Still alive, decades later.
