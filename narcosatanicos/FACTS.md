# The Narcosatanists (Matamoros, 1989) — factual notes
Episode 27 of **PLOT TWIST WITH A&R** (paper-cut lightbox, ~61s, English). Condensed from the
three-case script and fact-checked against Wikipedia ("Adolfo Constanzo", "Murder of Mark Kilroy").

## Documented
- **Mark Kilroy**, 21, a University of Texas pre-med student, disappeared in **Matamoros** during
  spring break in **March 1989** (sources give 13 or 14 March).
- On **1 April 1989**, **Serafín Hernández García** ran a drug checkpoint. Police followed him to
  **Rancho Santa Elena**.
- **Fifteen bodies** were dug up at the ranch, including Kilroy's. A ritual cauldron (nganga) was found.
- The leader was **Adolfo de Jesús Constanzo** ("El Padrino"), born 1962 in Miami. He told
  followers that ritual killings would protect them.
- On **6 May 1989**, cornered in **Mexico City**, he ordered a follower to shoot him.

## Corrected from the source script
- "27 bodies": the sources say 15 at the ranch, so the film says fifteen.
- "Believed he was invisible": this comes from popular accounts and is not in Wikipedia. The film
  says "according to later accounts", and the card reads "INVISIBLE"?
- "Held ~12 hours", "62 years for Sara Aldrete": the sources conflict or don't support these, so
  both were omitted.
- The graphic details (machete, brain) are deliberately left out.

## Depiction
No violence, remains or faces are shown. The dead appear as fifteen small flags. The missing
poster uses a blank silhouette, not a likeness.
