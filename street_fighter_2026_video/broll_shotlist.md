Note: this cloud session couldn't reach Pexels or Pixabay (blocked by the network), so these videos are fully animated in code instead. This list is optional extra B-roll.
Download the clips below yourself (free, no attribution required, though crediting is nice) and lay them
**under** the slides in CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Premiere: put the clip on a lower track and set the slide to *Screen* blend mode or ~85% opacity.
Never use real footage from the movie, its trailers or posters without permission. That's how videos get Content ID claims.
