Two years after Godzilla sank in Sagami Bay, Japan is still rebuilding. Koichi finally has a family. But his wife Noriko hides a secret: Godzilla's cells are changing her body.
Then a meteorite lands near Matsumoto. Out crawls a jelly-like creature that eats everything around it, until it grows wings and spits lightning. They call it Ghidorah.
While it sleeps, Koichi flies in with American support. A brand-new fuel-air bomb hits it dead-on. Ghidorah survives, and wipes out the escort planes.
So they try harpoons with lightning rods. The storm hits, and Ghidorah drops. Then it rises again, with three heads, two tails, and a beam that lifts whole buildings.
Off the island of Kozushima, the sea starts to boil. Godzilla has regenerated. Scientist Kanji Murakami wants the two monsters to fight.
But Murakami lost his family at Hiroshima. His secret plan is to steer Godzilla to New York, with a machine that only works through Noriko.
Koichi runs to hide her. But Noriko volunteers anyway, and Noda talks Murakami out of his revenge.
The battlefield is the ruins of Ginza. Godzilla tears off one of Ghidorah's heads. Ghidorah bites off his arm and buries him in rubble.
The team freezes Ghidorah with liquid nitrogen and soaks it in fuel. Noda triggers the trap by hand, and Koichi's rocket lights it. But the blast kills Noda.
Koichi and Noriko use the machine to wake Godzilla. But Ghidorah's lost head has swallowed Godzilla's cells. Now it has spines, and a heat ray of its own.
Beam against beam, Ghidorah loses control of its stolen fire, and Godzilla finishes it. The Americans let him go, because they want him as a weapon.
And if they want Godzilla, they'll want Noriko too. So the Shikishima family vanishes together. The war is over, but they still aren't free.
