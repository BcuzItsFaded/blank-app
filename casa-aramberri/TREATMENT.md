# La Casa de Aramberri: treatment

Style: Paper-cut Lightbox. 9:16 vertical, 30 fps, about 60 s, English narration (deep male read, brisk to fit 60 s), burned-in subtitles + .srt.

**Structure chosen:** eight lightbox dioramas, one per beat of the script, each with its own colour ladder. Alternatives considered: one continuous dolly down a single street (too static for a story with eight beats), and a case-file flip-through (loses the "lamp" idea). Chosen because the ladder change (cool night, warm dawn, red-black, amber gold, teal, slate, green, back to night) tells "another place, another truth" with no extra words.

**Logline:** a house on calle Aramberri, the murders that really happened, the legends that grew around them, and the line between the two.
**Arc:** curse (the house, one red window) → normal morning, warm → the murders, red, lights go out → motive, gold → the parrot legend → arrests, disputed → the haunting → the real twist: the box pulls out to a lamp on a table, the lamp goes dark.
**Native moves:** the lamp (windows lighting one by one); silhouette in a window; paper that writes itself (title strips); the box as keepsake (final pull-out and light-off).
**Benchmark (grammar only):** commercial paper-cut shadow-box lamps for the layer stack, rim light and cast shadows; Lotte Reiniger's silhouette language for the figures.
**Sound:** music-box lullaby in D minor over cellos; harp for the morning; tremolo strings for dread; silences at 19.0-20.0 s (after the lights go out), 40.0-40.45 s (before the "DISPUTED" stamp) and the end (lamp off).
**Camera:** slow dolly-ins, a lateral drift, a locked close frame, and the pull-out to the lamp.
**Titles:** cut-paper strips with glowing letters that grow from the left edge.
