# Facts and sources

Narration is the author's script, kept word for word. Claims checked against the sources below.

| Claim in the film | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Two women, Antonia Lozano and her daughter Florinda Montemayor, were murdered in a house on calle Aramberri, Monterrey, Nuevo León | Documented | UANL, "El crimen de la calle de Aramberri" (https://libros.uanl.mx/index.php/u/catalog/download/45/49/127?inline=1); TV Azteca, "Macabro crimen casa Arramberri" (https://www.tvazteca.com/azteca7/macabro-crimen-casa-arramberri); Posta, "Casa de Aramberri: ¿cuál es la historia de este lugar?" (https://www.posta.com.mx/nuevoleon/casa-de-aramberri-cual-es-la-historia-de-este-lugar/vl1621876) |
| Date: April 5, 1933 | Documented (consistent across the sources above) | same |
| Both women were stabbed to death; a double murder that shocked the city | Documented in the case materials as given by the author; no graphic detail is shown in the film | UANL book above |
| Motive: robbery; thousands of pesos in gold "reportedly" the target | Documented as the investigated motive; the amount is reported, not verified | UANL book above (author-supplied: Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León materials; Excélsior, "The Crime and the Legend") |
| The parrot repeated a cry that "supposedly" identified a killer | Legend, disputed, and framed as such in the film (a "DISPUTED" stamp) | Folklore accounts; the film does not repeat any quoted words |
| Suspects were identified; the real investigation was "far more complicated" | The identity of the culprits differs between sources (a relative and accomplices, or two nephews), so the film names nobody | Posta / TV Azteca / UANL, accounts differ |
| Screams, footsteps and ghostly figures in the abandoned house | Folklore, never proven, framed as such ("some claim", "stories spread", "never proven") | Folklore accounts |
| The film's visual details (facade, parrot, gold, line-up, lamp) | Illustrative paper-cut art, not depictions of the real house or people | n/a |

The victims were real people. The film treats the murders without gore and separates the documented case from the legends.
