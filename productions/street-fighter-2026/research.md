## Theories (S18, clearly labelled on screen)

1. Bison absorbs Akuma's power.
2. Sagat allies with Bison for revenge; Ryu vs Sagat rematch.
3. Ryu's dark side is dormant, not gone.
4. Gouken returns (he survives Akuma in the games' continuity).
5. Cammy (mind-control arc in the games), Zangief, E. Honda get bigger roles.
