# La Planchada: treatment

Style: 1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon. 9:16 vertical (the 4:3 gate of the style is widened to fill a portrait frame, with the same rounded gate, weave, flicker, scratches and vignette), 24 fps, 62.4 s (the last 3.8 s hold the ending card), English narration in a radio-announcer chain, title-card subtitles + .srt.

**Structure chosen:** a haunted hospital as a hot-jazz cartoon: eight short gags, one per beat of the script, with the whole building in black and white and the ghost nurse the only character who glides. Considered: a bouncing-ball singalong about the legend (too jokey for a sympathetic ghost) and a single long tracking shot down the corridor (loses the legend's "versions"). Chosen because rubber-hose horror lets the legend be eerie and tender at once.

**Logline:** a night nurse who loves her patients too much to leave them.
**Arc:** corridor at midnight → a perfectly pressed uniform (and a flat-iron with a face) → the betrayal in a flashback → two versions of her death, the second ending in a rubber-band "unable to rest" gag → she tends a sick patient → morning: nobody knows her → the portrait with a black ribbon and a patient who bolts → she tucks in another patient; a heart floats up → ending card.
**Native moves:** the whole world breathing on the beat; rubber-hose stretch (the ghost snapping back); an object character (the iron); the film as an object (flashback iris, window-shade ending card); the band stopping dead on the stare and on the double-take.
**Benchmark (grammar only):** early Fleischer-era shorts for the breathing world and transformation; Cuphead for finishing and scoring. No existing character is used.
**Sound:** minor-key foxtrot: tuba oom-pah, stride piano, ukulele, muted trumpet, trombone smears, clarinet and xylophone themes; slide whistles and boings by material; three band stops (the stare at 40.6 s, the freeze at 49.1 s, the heart-break at 21.3 s). Voice through a radio chain; everything run through an optical-soundtrack pass (band-limit, wow and flutter, hiss).
**Titles:** Shrikhand title, Limelight sub-line, IM Fell English SC announcer plates.
