# VISUAL CONTINUITY BIBLE — DON'T SPOIL IT RG

**One consistent art direction:** 1990s hand-drawn Japanese anime horror; hand-painted stormy skies, aged cel acetate texture, heavy ink blacks, physically plausible scale, realistic facial anatomy, restrained film grain, amber recovery scenes vs desaturated steel-blue kaiju attacks.

**Godzilla:** Keep a single towering bipedal silhouette, charcoal reptilian skin, massively muscular hind legs, uneven jagged dorsal plates, long heavy tail, familiar reptilian jaw, huge scale. Dorsal plates glow *pale blue* only for heat-ray preparation. No redesign between shots, no extra heads/limbs.

**Koichi Shikishima:** Adult Japanese man, neat 1940s dark hair, lean face, dark work jacket, thoughtful and protective expression.

**Noriko Shikishima:** Adult Japanese woman, black shoulder-length 1940s hair, pale beige clothing, a small mysterious dark mark on the neck as continuity with the previous film. The cause is **not** asserted.

**Akiko:** Young Japanese girl, short dark hair, modest period dress, frightened but unharmed; no graphic peril.

**Doctor Ogata:** Mature Japanese doctor, graying hair, period-accurate clinical clothing and paper notes. Do not identify his quoted female patient as Noriko unless confirmed by a direct film source.

**Kenji Noda:** Technical strategist; dark 1940s suit, battle-weary look, paper maps and hand-drawn charts.

**Kanji Murakami:** Elder Japanese biologist with war trauma; lab sketches and specimen charts; avoid treating unshown research scenes as actual film footage.

**Makoto Aoyama:** Japanese woman weather specialist, period-accurate clothes, analog weather charts; weather office is invented staging.

**Places:** 1949 Japan, Tokyo-like rebuilt wooden neighborhoods, surviving tram wires, paper notices, analog radio/phones; never depict contemporary Japan. Use tiny humans / low camera to establish believable Godzilla scale.
