# Keyframe asset prompts (9:16, 1080×1920)

The video is a code-drawn animatic. For final-quality art, generate one painted plate
per scene with these prompts, save each one as `public/keyframes/scene-XX.png`, and set
`"keyframe": "keyframes/scene-XX.png"` for that scene in `src/scenes.json`. The plate is
drawn as the scene's base layer, and the same camera move and parallax are applied to it.
The particles, captions, fact tags and brand mark stay on top.

**Shared style prefix (paste before every prompt):**
> Hand-painted 1990s Japanese anime horror cel, 9:16 vertical, realistic human anatomy and proportions,
> painted watercolor-gouache backgrounds of postwar 1949 Japan, heavy chiaroscuro shadows, ink linework
> with slight hand-drawn wobble, film grain, muted desaturated palette with cold blue atomic accents,
> cinematic composition, no text, no logos, no toys, no LEGO, no chibi, no mascot or host character.

**Character consistency sheet (keep identical across prompts):**
- **Koichi Shikishima**: Japanese man, late 20s, gaunt, short black hair with a loose fringe, haunted eyes, dark work jacket over a white open-collar shirt.
- **Noriko Shikishima**: Japanese woman, mid 20s, chin-length black bob with bangs, pale cream blouse, a dark bruise-like mark with thin black veins on the left side of her neck.
- **Kenji Noda**: Japanese man, 40s, neat side-parted hair, round wire glasses, rumpled brown suit and tie.
- **Kanji Murakami**: elderly Japanese biologist, 70s, cropped grey hair, deeply lined gaunt face, white lab coat. This is an original look and not a likeness of the actor.
- **Godzilla**: towering charcoal-black reptilian titan, keloid scar-textured hide, small fierce eyes under heavy brow ridges, three rows of jagged maple-leaf dorsal plates that glow ice-blue when charging. Original design, not the official film model.
