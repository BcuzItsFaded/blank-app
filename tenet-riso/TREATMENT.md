# Tenet, printed: a risograph treatment

Style: Risograph Print (`STYLE.md` is fixed). Frame 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 125 s.
Narration: the same 15 voiced lines as the colored-pencil cut (the user's ~280-word script, English, male storyteller), with subtitles on a paper-white knockout strip.

## 1. Three candidate structures

A. **Plates as narrative.** Time is the ink: forward time is one plate, inverted time another, and the trinket and the Algorithm a third. Each turn of the story adds or removes a plate; where forward meets inverted, the inks overprint to near-black.
B. **Subtraction.** Open in full overprint (all three plates) and lose a plate at each loss, ending in one colour. Strong ending, but the plot's logic (backward moves, the founder reveal) would be reversed in the image.
C. **A zine.** Each shot is a page with a different two-plate pairing, turned in order. Good for a series, weak for a single story with a twist.

**Chosen: A, with B's ending.** The three inks carry the film's logic: blue is the forward world (the agent, Neil, the freeport); pink is the inverted world (inverted bullets, inverted gold, reversed cars, the inverted soldiers); sunflower is the thing both worlds want (the Algorithm, then the trinket). The turnstile is the overprint of blue and pink, which is the moment the two directions of time meet. The last shot removes the blue and pink plates, leaving only the sunflower trinket: the founder's mark.

## 2. Logline and arc

One sentence: a secret war fought in two directions of time is printed plate by plate, until the one plate left is the trinket that started it.

- **Setup (0:00–0:20):** the opera house as a paper knockout that its blue plate fills; the masked soldier and his sunflower trinket; the backward bullet; the loyalty test.
- **Turn (0:20–0:50):** inversion as halftone dots shrinking; pink gold arriving from the future; the nine rings of the Algorithm, each one printed with its own drum "ka-chunk"; the team forms.
- **Escalation (0:50–1:25):** the freeport crash (blue meets the warehouse; the overprint); the highway heist (one pink car reverses); Kat is shot with a pink backward bullet and her blue plate lifts; the turnstile is the overprint; the yacht and the switch.
- **Climax and twist (1:25–2:00):** the pincer as two registration-drifting groups (blue forward, pink inverted); the yacht's blue plate lifts (implied); the Algorithm pulled out, ring by ring; the inverted soldier's pink figure drops into a knockout; the trinket alone; the print on the wall.

## 3. Benchmark

Learn: the overprint as a meaning (two directions of time making a third colour), the knockout reveal, plate arrival with a roller sweep, the registration kick on accents, halftone scale, the one-ink ending.
Don't learn: the demo's Sunday Ride story, its characters, its shots, its ink choices, its score, its end card.

## 4. Characters, places, copyright

- Every character is a **fictional stand-in**: a silhouette with no likeness and no costume from the film. Names appear only in the narration and subtitles, as the user's script gives them.
- Places are generic: an opera house, a warehouse, a highway, a vault, a blast-site dome, a yacht, a turnstile. No recognisable real buildings, aircraft liveries or logos.
- The title is printed as the user's words, in a grotesque, not the film's logo or type.
- No RISO brand, logo or machine trade dress; the inks are named only by colour.

## 5. Inks (two spot plates + one, never more)

| Plate | Ink (colour reference) | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | Blue `#0078BF` | forward time: the agent, Neil, Kat, the freeport, the cars |
| Pink | Fluorescent Pink `#FF48B0` | inverted time: inverted bullets, inverted gold, reversed cars, inverted soldiers |
| Sunflower | Sunflower `#FFB511` | the object both directions want: the Algorithm, then the trinket |
| Paper | `#F4EEE2` | knockouts, the subtitle strip, highlights |

Overprints: blue + pink = near-black violet (the turnstile, the impact); pink + sunflower = warm red (the Algorithm meeting inverted time); blue + sunflower = dark green (the yacht's switch).

## 8. Cue map

- Tempo: 84 bpm in section 1, 96 in section 2, 116 in section 3 (the heist), 72 with a pulse in section 4, back to 84 for the trinket.
- The score layers like plates: upright bass (section 1), vibraphone added (section 2), brushed kit and shaker (section 3, the heist), toy piano and nylon guitar (section 4). Each instrument leaves as its plate lifts at the end.
- Cuts land on plate arrivals: a drum "ka-chunk" on the Algorithm's rings and on the overprint at the freeport impact; the registration kick on the trinket.
- Silence: a held bar with the reverb cut, once, at the turnstile's match cut, then the snap back.

## 10. Subtitles and titles

- Subtitles: English only, on a paper-white knockout strip with text on one plate (~44 px, geometric sans), held for at least the line's speech length + 0.6 s.
- Title: heavy grotesque, double-hit (two plates with a visible offset).
- Labels: at most one per shot, in the same grotesque.

## 11. Self-check against the demo

Before delivery, the opening (a knockout of the opera house, not a title on a field), the structure (three inks as directions of time), the signature shot (the turnstile's overprint match cut), the score (instruments that layer and leave like plates) and the ending (one ink left, then the print on a wall) must differ from the demo in at least four of six ways.

## Revision: more detail (shot by shot, with detailed characters)

The first cut piled every beat onto one sheet. This version gives each narration beat its own sheet (23 shots), and the plates roll in again at every cut with a new misregistration, like a new sheet through the drum.

Characters are original stand-ins built from one rig (no likeness of any actor), each told apart by costume, hair and ink:
- **The Protagonist**: close crop, stubble, dark suit with tie, white cuffs, watch; tactical kit with helmet in the opera and at Stalsk-12. Blue.
- **Neil**: swept hair, linen jacket with creases, rolled sleeves, striped scarf, backpack carrying the orange trinket. Blue, turning pink as he walks back.
- **Kat**: low bun, earrings, belted trench coat with epaulettes. Blue; at the shooting she fades to a paper outline (implied).
- **Andrei Sator**: heavy build, receding grey hair, stern brows, stubble, double-breasted overcoat, a sunflower signet ring. Overprinted blue + pink, the darkest ink in the film; pink with a breathing mask when inverted.
- **The masked soldier**: helmet, gas mask with filter, plate carrier with pouches, rifle, pack with the trinket. Blue in the opera, pink (inverted) at the gate.
- **The scientist**: bob, glasses, lab coat with lanyard badge. **The handler**: cap, glasses, peacoat.

Shots: title clock running backward; opera exterior (columns, statues, lit arches, vans, assault team); opera interior (balconies, curtain, chandelier, standoff); the bullet flying back into the gun; rail-yard loyalty test with the pill magnified; recruitment at sea with TENET mirrored; inversion lab; Sator with inverted gold and a radio mast receiving pink waves; the nine parts on a blueprint; the planet turning pink; the team line-up; jet into the freeport; the highway with a pink car driving backward; inverted Sator, Kat, the final piece; the turnstile (blue room / pink room); the yacht with a dead-man's switch inset (water reflection on); Stalsk-12 cutaway with the Algorithm under a bomb; the temporal pincer (a building collapsing on one side and reassembling on the other); the Algorithm pulled out / meanwhile on the yacht; the inverted soldier at the gate; the trinket on Neil's bag in profile close-up; Neil's farewell; THE FOUNDER over the loop of stations. ## Revision 3: better characters

The character rig was rebuilt, not just dressed up:
- **Faces:** a real skull, jaw and chin (wider jowls for Sator, a narrower jaw for Kat); eyes with iris, pupil, highlight and lids (blinking); brows that change with mood; a nose with a shadow side and nostril; a mouth that is calm, smiling, grinning, smirking or worried; ears; stubble that follows the jaw.
- **Each person has a face of their own:** the Protagonist (flat stern brows, chin cleft), Sator (heavy brows, large nose, eye bags, smirk), Neil (soft brows, a grin), Kat (arched brows, lashes, lipstick).
- **Bodies:** sloped shoulders, a waist, a shorter neck with a shirt collar; a feminine silhouette for Kat (narrow shoulders, belted waist, flared coat); elbow creases and sleeve folds.
- **Hands** have a palm, four fingers and a thumb; gloves for the soldiers.
- **Colour per plate:** pink cheeks, lips, nose tip and ears overprinted on the blue skin; a violet tie (blue under pink); a sunflower scarf, watch, belt buckle and earrings.
- **Staging:** a ground shadow under every standing figure; expressive poses in the portraits (a hand to the cuff, to the lapel, to the strap, arms folded); the trinket close-up now shows the Protagonist's face looking at the bag.
