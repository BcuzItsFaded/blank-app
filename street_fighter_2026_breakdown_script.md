# Street Fighter (2026) — Animated Movie Breakdown
**Format:** YouTube long-form, ~3 min, 16:9
**Style:** 1990s action anime + 2D arcade fighting game: hand-drawn cel shading, film grain, speed lines, dramatic close-ups, impact frames, health bars, and "ROUND 1 / K.O." callouts
**Narration:** One energetic narrator, plus arcade-announcer stingers

> Character look rule: each fighter is recognizable from the film's version through costume, silhouette, hair and colors, redrawn as anime characters. Don't reproduce the actors' faces photorealistically, and don't use studio logos.

---

## Cold open (0:00–0:12)
**Visual:** Black screen. A coin drops and an arcade CRT flickers on. Rain on a neon street, 1993. Two silhouettes in torn gis face each other. Impact frame: "STREET FIGHTER (2026)".
**Narration:** "Thirty years after the arcade changed everything, Street Fighter is back on the big screen. Here's the breakdown."
**Announcer:** "ROUND ONE… FIGHT!"

## 1. The brothers (0:12–0:40)
**Visual:** A sepia flashback to the 1987 tournament. Young Ryu (white gi, red headband, stoic) and Ken (red gi, blond hair, cocky grin) fight back to back, then trade blows in the final. Ryu lands the winning Shoryuken in a frozen impact frame. Ken walks away and the scene splits down the middle.
**Narration:** "Ryu and Ken trained like brothers. Then Ryu won the 1987 tournament, and they went their separate ways."

## 2. The recruit (0:40–1:05)
**Visual:** 1993. Chun-Li (blue qipao, ox-horn hair buns, spiked bracelets) finds Ryu at a wandering-fighter dojo, then tracks Ken to a flashy underground fight. Close-ups of her eyes, with a Spinning Bird Kick in the background.
**Narration:** "Chun-Li needs them both. A new World Warrior Tournament is coming, and it's hiding something."

## 3. The roster (1:05–1:45) — character select screen
**Visual:** A 90s-style select grid. Each portrait snaps in with a mini move clip:
- **Guile:** tall flat-top, green fatigues, Sonic Boom
- **Blanka:** green skin, wild orange mane, electric charge
- **Dhalsim:** stretching limbs, Yoga Fire
- **Balrog:** boxer, blue and red trunks, Dash Punch
- **Akuma:** dark gi, red hair, glowing eyes; shadows and flames only, kept as a tease
**Narration:** "And what a roster: Guile, Blanka, Dhalsim, Balrog… and a dark presence that every fan will recognize."

## 4. The villain (1:45–2:10)
**Visual:** Low angle. M. Bison in his red Shadaloo uniform and cape, with a skull-emblem cap and violet Psycho Power crackling. Slow push-in on the glowing eyes. Thunder.
**Narration:** "Behind it all is M. Bison. He's running the tournament, and the stakes are the whole world."

## 5. The clash (2:10–2:40)
**Visual:** Fast-cut fight montage: Ryu vs. Ken face-off, health bars draining, a simultaneous Hadoken collision filling the screen with white light, Chun-Li vs. Bison, Guile's Flash Kick.
**Narration:** "To stop him, Ryu and Ken have to face the tournament, the conspiracy, and each other."

## 6. The verdict (2:40–3:00)
**Visual:** Stylized "critic scorecard" UI with power bars. Cast and action are high; plot and editing are lower.
**Narration:** "Critics are split. They love the cast, especially Andrew Koji's Ryu and Callina Liang's Chun-Li, and the over-the-top fights. They're less kind to the rushed plot and choppy editing. Final score: a fun, flawed round."
**Announcer:** "K.O.!" Freeze frame of Ryu and Ken fist-bumping, then "CONTINUE? 10… 9…" and a subscribe prompt.
