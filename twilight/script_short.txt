The biggest battle in Twilight never actually happened. Spoilers!

In Breaking Dawn Part Two, the Volturi face the Cullens in the snow. Carlisle loses his head. Jasper, Seth, and Leah fall. Bella and Edward take down Aro.

Then the twist: it was only a vision. Alice showed Aro what would happen if they fought, so he walked away.

And in the book? There's no battle at all. Just talking, a mind shield, and the Volturi leaving.
