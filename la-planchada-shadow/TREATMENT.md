# La Planchada (shadow puppetry): treatment

Style: Shadow Puppetry. 9:16 vertical, 24 fps, 62.4 s (the last 3.4 s hold the ending plaque), English narration, a storyteller's placard for subtitles.

**Structure chosen:** the same eight beats played as one night's show on a lit cloth. Considered: a single procession pan (too static for eight beats) and a backstage-first reveal of the puppeteers (the legend is already about someone hidden). Chosen because the medium is itself a ghost story: flat figures made of light, moved by someone you cannot see.

**Logline:** a night nurse who loves her patients too much to leave them.
**Arc:** the show begins from the audience seat, the title plaque is pressed on and lifted off → she shuffles down a corridor of lit doors → a flat-iron on a rod presses her skirt, the lamp flares → a memory under more lamps: doctor, hearts on rods, the betrayal, a heart split in two → two versions of her death (rain strips; her ghost lifted off the cloth and pulled back on a thread) → she tends a patient and a new lamp flares with her comfort → morning, bleached by four lamps; nobody knows her → a portrait pressed on, a freeze, a flight → she tucks in a patient, a red heart on a rod rises; the lamp is carried away and the cloth darkens → the ending plaque pressed on.
**Native moves:** the light is the world (lamp count and flares carry mood); off the screen = big and blurry (title, ghost, question marks); carved and translucent (holes glow, overlaps darken); hand-held tremor; piece swap and lift-off.
**Benchmark (grammar only):** Chinese shadow theatre (carved dyed hide, lamp, rods) and Lotte Reiniger's profile acting. No existing puppet or story is copied.
**Sound:** percussion first: barrel drum (timpani) and clapper (woodblock) patterns, gongs on decisive hits, an erhu-like fiddle, bamboo flute and a plucked zither; hard silences at 21.0-21.5 s and 46.7-49.1 s (with one zither harmonic inside the second).
**Rendering note:** a Canvas 2D compositor: every piece is drawn with multiply onto a white transmission canvas, multiplied by a flickering lamp field and a woven cloth, then bloom and a warm vignette.
