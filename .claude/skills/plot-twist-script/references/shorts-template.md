# 90-second Shorts narration template (one entry)

Target: 200–230 spoken words, 10 vertical (9:16) comic panels, ~9 seconds each.

```
<Title> — Ending Explained in 90 Seconds
PlotTwistWith R&A

Panel 1 | 0:00–0:09 | HOOK
Narration: <one shocking line — a question or a reveal that makes viewers stay>
Caption: <3–6 words on screen>
Image prompt: <vertical comic panel, who/what/where, mood, lighting>

Panel 2 | 0:09–0:18 | HOOK → SETUP
Panel 3 | 0:18–0:27 | SUMMARY
Panel 4 | 0:27–0:36 | SUMMARY
Panel 5 | 0:36–0:45 | TWIST build-up
Panel 6 | 0:45–0:54 | TWIST
Panel 7 | 0:54–1:03 | TWIST fallout
Panel 8 | 1:03–1:12 | ENDING
Panel 9 | 1:12–1:21 | ENDING explained / what it means
Panel 10 | 1:21–1:30 | CLOSING QUESTION
Narration: <ask viewers something to comment on, e.g. "Was Katniss right to shoot Coin?">
Caption: <the question, short>
```

Rules:
- Every panel has all three lines: Narration, Caption, Image prompt.
- Keep the same character descriptions in every image prompt.
- Narration lines are short and spoken-style: contractions, no semicolons.
- End with a question, never with "like and subscribe" alone.
