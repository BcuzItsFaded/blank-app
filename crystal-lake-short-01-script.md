# Crystal Lake Short #1 — "The Killer Was Never Jason"

**Voice:** Low, calm, campfire-storyteller narrator (male or female), slight whisper on key lines

## Narration (exact script — ~140 words)

> The killer in Friday the 13th… was never Jason.
>
> Summer, 1957. Camp Crystal Lake. A quiet boy named Jason goes into the water.
>
> The counselors who should be watching him… aren't.
>
> He never comes back up.
>
> His mother, Pamela, loses everything that day. The camp moves on. The town forgets. She doesn't.
>
> For years she carries it. The grief. The blame. And a voice in her head that sounds like her son.
>
> *"Kill her, Mommy."*
>
> So when the camp tries to reopen… Pamela comes back. And one by one, the counselors disappear.
>
> That's the twist of the original film: the first killer was a mother.
>
> Jason didn't pick up the machete until the sequels.
>
> Peacock's *Crystal Lake* tells Pamela's story — how a mom becomes a monster.
>
> So… who's really to blame? 👇
