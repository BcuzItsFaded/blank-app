# La Planchada (paper pop-up book): treatment

Style: Paper Pop-up Book. 9:16 vertical, 24 fps, 62.4 s (the last 3.4 s hold the closed back cover), English narration, cream-on-brown storybook subtitles.

**Structure chosen:** a hardcover book on a desk opens and each beat is a spread whose cut-paper set pops up back to front; the page turns between spreads; the book closes on the ending text. Considered: one long fold-out pop-up (one setting only) and a pull-tab book where every beat is a tab (too much to read in 60 s). Chosen because turning the pages is itself a story rhythm and the book's cover gives a title and an ending screen for free.

**Logline:** a night nurse who loves her patients too much to leave them.
**Arc:** closed red cover with a foil title → opens onto a midnight corridor → a pressed uniform and a hopping flat-iron → a green-and-gold memory with red hearts, split in two → two grey/indigo spreads for the two versions of her death (rain; a ghost pulled back on a string) → a ward at night, a new tenderness → a yellow morning and three question marks → a portrait with a ribbon, a jump and a run → the ward again, a red heart pops up; the book closes on THE NURSE IS A LEGEND.
**Native moves:** pop-ups rising back to front with overshoot; flat cut-paper actors with white borders and cardboard edges; cut-out shadows; the page turn as the transition; the desk with a lamp and soft blurred props around the book.
**Benchmark (grammar only):** layered pop-up books and the flat-actor-in-a-diorama look of paper-craft games. No existing character is used.
**Colour:** one dominant hue family per spread (indigo, teal, meadow, graphite, ward blue, morning ochre) with red hearts as the recurring accent; the death spread is graphite on cream, as an "outside the story" page.
**Sound:** toy-like and acoustic: glockenspiel (music box), pizzicato bass and ukulele, clarinet, toy-piano-like upright; paper foley is the signature (page swish, pop-up snaps with a short pitch sweep, taps for steps, cardboard thumps, the creak of the cover); silences at 21.0-21.5 s and 46.7-49.1 s.
**Rendering note:** a Canvas 2D 2.5D version of the style (cards drawn with a border, edge and grain, hinged at their base) because the container has no GPU for the library's 3D path.
