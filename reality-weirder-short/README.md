# Reality is weirder than science fiction (30 s facts Short)

The script is the user's, verbatim, read by Kokoro `am_michael` and fitted to 30 s. The art is original cel animation:
- Earth and a UFO under "REALITY > SCI-FI"
- a ground clock and an orbit clock that runs faster (+38 µs/day), a GPS route and an orbiting satellite
- Venus completing its orbit (225 days) while it spins less than once (243-day rotation)
- the T. rex turning into a pigeon that steals your fries
- the closing line: "Sounds fake. It's reality."

Sources:
- **GPS:** the relativity correction is ~38 µs/day (gravity +45, speed −7). See Ashby, *Living Reviews in Relativity*.
- **Venus:** it rotates once in about 243 Earth days, and its year is about 224.7 Earth days. See Astronomy.com and Royal Museums Greenwich.
- **Birds:** birds are living (avian) dinosaurs. See the American Museum of Natural History, "Birds: Living Dinosaurs".

```bash
npm ci && npm run audio && npm run render   # -> out/reality-weirder-short.mp4
```
