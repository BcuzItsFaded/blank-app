# La Planchada (paper-cut lightbox): treatment

Style: Paper-cut Lightbox. 9:16 vertical, 30 fps, 62.4 s, English narration, serif subtitles on the dark front sheet, title and ending lines as glowing cut-paper strips.

**Structure chosen:** eight dioramas in one shallow box, each with its own colour ladder (back sheets bright and translucent, front sheets dark and opaque), lit from behind; the film ends by pulling out of the box to a lamp on a table that is switched off. Considered: a single long scroll through the hospital (too uniform) and a figure-free film told only with lit windows (the nurse should be seen). Chosen because a lightbox makes a ghost literal: she is the only sheet that is nearly white and glowing, while the living are dark paper silhouettes.

**Logline:** a night nurse who loves her patients too much to leave them.
**Arc:** an amber-lit corridor at midnight, the ghost walking forward → the pressed uniform, creases drawn as straight lines, lamp flares as the iron presses → a warm sunset memory with red heart sheets, split in two → a grey page for Version A, an indigo ward for Version B (a spirit pulled back by a thread) → the night visit, the window turning from moonlight to warm gold as she comforts → morning bleached by light, question marks as glowing strips → a portrait that glows, a jump, a run → a red heart rises over a bed; the box is pulled out to a lit lamp, the strips read THE NURSE IS A LEGEND. and the lamp goes out.
**Native moves:** the lamp turns on (windows and doors burn amber); light-through-paper (the ghost is a translucent sheet with a bloom); depth as distance (parallax between sheets); the box as keepsake (the closing pull-out).
**Benchmark (grammar only):** commercial paper-cut shadow-box lamps and Lotte Reiniger's silhouette figures. No existing character is used.
**Sound:** music box (glockenspiel) over felt piano, soft strings and harp; paper rustles on every strip, switch clicks and shimmers when lights come on, soft thumps, a single lamp click at the end; silences at 21.0-21.5 s and 46.7-49.1 s, and the final one when the lamp goes out.
**Rendering note:** a Canvas 2D emulation: sheets baked with fibre grain, a warm top rim and a dark bottom band, composited back to front with cast shadows, backlight glow and bloom.
