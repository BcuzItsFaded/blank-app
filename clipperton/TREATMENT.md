# CLIPPERTON, 1917 — treatment
PLOT TWIST WITH A&R · Episode 24 · vertical 9:16 · ~61s · paper-cut lightbox · English

**Look.** Cut paper. A ring of coral and palms at sunset, with the dark rock and its lighthouse.
Warm colony afternoons give way to grey empty horizons. At night the lighthouse beam becomes
the menace, and dawn brings the Yorktown's silhouette. Orange land crabs and the Mexican
tricolor are the colour notes. A small A&R badge sits in the corner throughout.

1. **0:00 Hook.** An empty horizon at dusk and the atoll's lighthouse beam. Cards: CLIPPERTON /
   LA ISLA DONDE EL ÚLTIMO HOMBRE / SE CONVIRTIÓ EN UNA AMENAZA / A DOCUMENTED TRUE STORY.
2. **0:05 The colony.** Huts, palms, soldiers, families and the flag. CLIPPERTON ISLAND ·
   PACIFIC OCEAN / 1,000+ KM FROM ACAPULCO / CAPTAIN RAMÓN ARNAUD.
3. **0:16 The ships stopped coming.** A mother and child on a grey beach as the crabs swarm.
   HUNGER · SCURVY.
4. **0:23 They never came back.** A boat rows toward a ship on the horizon, and the swell swallows it.
5. **0:29 The lighthouse keeper.** Under the moon, the last man stands on the gallery as the
   beam sweeps, and a gold crown appears. VICTORIANO ÁLVAREZ / "KING OF CLIPPERTON".
6. **0:37 Nowhere to run.** The beam sweeps the huts and his shadow crosses the wall. ONLY OCEAN.
7. **0:44 July 18, 1917.** Two women hand in hand at dawn, and the lighthouse goes dark.
   ALICIA ARNAUD · TIRZA RENDÓN.
8. **0:49 That same day.** The USS Yorktown steams in, and the children wave. 11 SURVIVORS /
   HELP CAME THE DAY THEY STOPPED WAITING / PLOT TWIST WITH A&R.

**Sound.** Surf on the reef throughout, with gulls and wind in the palms. A gentle nylon-guitar
waltz with flute plays for the colony's good days, then sours to minor as the ships stop. The
crabs click, oars creak, and a wave takes the boat. The lighthouse clockwork ticks over low
dread and a slow heartbeat. A steam whistle carries across the water as the waltz returns in
major. Mastered to −14 LUFS.
