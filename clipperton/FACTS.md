# Clipperton, 1917 — factual notes

Episode 24 of **PLOT TWIST WITH A&R** (paper-cut lightbox, ~61s, English narration).
On-screen title (Spanish): *Clipperton: la isla donde el último hombre se convirtió en una amenaza.*
**Documented history, not a ghost story.**

## Documented
- **Clipperton** is a remote coral atoll in the eastern Pacific, more than 1,000 km from
  Acapulco. In the early 1900s it was held by Mexico, with a small garrison of soldiers and
  their families under **Captain Ramón Arnaud**.
- The colony depended on supply ships. During the **Mexican Revolution** the ships stopped
  coming. Food ran out and **scurvy** killed many.
- When a ship was sighted, **Arnaud and the remaining soldiers rowed out to reach it and
  drowned**. Accounts give different years for this.
- The only adult man left was the lighthouse keeper, **Victoriano Álvarez**. He declared himself
  **"king of Clipperton"** and terrorized the women and girls. Survivors' accounts describe rape,
  beatings and murder.
- On **18 July 1917**, **Alicia Rovira de Arnaud** (the captain's widow) and **Tirza Rendón**
  (also spelled Randon) killed him. Accounts differ on who struck the blow, so the narration
  names both.
- **That same day** the US gunboat **USS Yorktown** arrived, on a wartime patrol, not in answer
  to any call for help. It took off **three women and eight children**, eleven survivors in all,
  who went back to Mexico.

## Depiction
- The narration names the abuse in a single factual line ("rape, beatings, and murder") and
  shows none of it. Álvarez appears only as a silhouette and a passing shadow. The killing is
  never shown: the lighthouse light simply goes out.
