Years of rivalry led to this moment! Ash versus Gary, at the Johto League!
Gary's Nidoqueen defeats Tauros, but Ash's Heracross strikes back against Magmar!
Then Blastoise enters! Its overwhelming power takes down both Heracross and Bayleef!
Snorlax defeats Arcanine and Nidoqueen, but Gary's Scizor turns the battle around!
Ash's final Pokémon is Charizard! It defeats Scizor and Golem, leaving Gary one last challenger!
Blastoise returns! Its powerful Hydro Pump gives Gary a massive type advantage!
Charizard scorches the battlefield! Blastoise cools the rocks, filling the arena with thick steam!
Out of the steam, Charizard unleashes Seismic Toss! Blastoise falls! Ash finally defeats Gary!
