# The 26 Deaths at Mazorra — factual notes

Episode 20 of **PLOT TWIST WITH A&R** (paper-cut lightbox, ~61s, English). A documented
true tragedy, not a legend. No ghosts or invented incidents are presented as fact.

## Documented
- **Where:** the Havana Psychiatric Hospital, Cuba's largest mental hospital, popularly
  called **Mazorra**.
- **When:** in **January 2010** (about 11–12 January), during an unusual cold spell,
  **26 patients died**. Cuba's health ministry confirmed the toll. Most outlets
  report 26, though some Cuban independent media gave higher figures.
- **Conditions:** reports cited hypothermia together with malnutrition and vitamin
  deficiencies, plus missing blankets and warm clothing and broken windows. The
  narration flags the windows with "reportedly", as these came from witness and
  independent-media accounts.
- **The investigation:** it found negligence and the diversion of food and supplies
  meant for patients.
- **The trial (January 2011):** prosecutors sought 6 to 14 years. AFP reported
  **13** workers jailed, and AP described **more than a dozen** officials and employees
  convicted, with sentences of about 5 to 15 years. The **director, Wilfredo Castillo
  Donate, received 15 years**.

## Script correction made for this film
The original script said "fourteen ... were sentenced" and "some received fifteen years".
Sources don't support a count of fourteen, and only the director is reported
with 15 years. The narration and cards were changed to "**more than a dozen** ... were
sentenced" and "**the hospital's director received fifteen years**".

## Depiction
- Patients are shown only as faceless silhouettes huddled on bare beds. No bodies or
  violence are shown, and the deaths are marked only by figures fading and a tally of 26.
- The "monster" shadow is a visual metaphor that the narration rejects.
