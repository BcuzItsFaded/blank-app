# THE 26 DEATHS AT MAZORRA — treatment
PLOT TWIST WITH A&R · Episode 20 · vertical 9:16 · ~61s · paper-cut lightbox · English

**Look.** Cold cut paper in deep indigo and icy blue light. Havana's royal palms
bend in the wind. The only warm light in the film is the 26 memorial candles
at the end. Patients are never shown with faces, only as huddled silhouettes on
bare iron beds.

1. **0:00 Hook.** The colonnaded hospital at night, with palms whipping in a cold wind.
   Cards: THE 26 DEATHS / AT MAZORRA / A DOCUMENTED TRUE STORY.
2. **0:06 Havana, January 2010.** The Capitolio and the Malecón, with spray bursting
   over the seawall. Card: A RARE COLD FRONT. Then closer on Mazorra.
3. **0:16 The cold.** A ward with broken arched windows, torn curtains, iron beds,
   no blankets, and breath fogging in the air.
4. **0:24 One after another.** The figures fade from their beds one by one while a
   chalk tally on the wall counts to 26. Card: 26 PATIENTS DEAD.
5. **0:31 Corruption.** An investigator's flashlight sweeps empty shelves while,
   through the doorway, sacks are carried out to a truck.
6. **0:40 The trial, 2011.** The scales of justice, the judge, the gavel, then cell bars
   slam down. Cards: OVER A DOZEN SENTENCED / THE DIRECTOR: 15 YEARS.
7. **0:47 The twist.** A horned, clawed monster's shadow looms on the corridor wall.
   The lamp flickers, and it becomes the shadow of an ordinary man holding a clipboard.
   Cards: NO MONSTER / THE HORROR WAS HUMAN.
8. **0:54 In memory.** Before the hospital at first light, 26 candles are lit one by one.
   Cards: 26 LIVES / PLOT TWIST WITH A&R.

**Sound.** The score is a sparse D-minor piano elegy over a cold cello drone and icy
tremolo strings. Foley: wind howling through the broken panes, ticking glass, flapping
curtains, the sea at the Malecón, a chalk stroke for each of the 26, a flashlight click,
an idling truck, the gavel, the bars clanging, a buzzing lamp that flickers, and matches
struck for the candles. The wind dies down at the memorial. Mastered to −14 LUFS.
