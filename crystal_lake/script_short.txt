Peacock's Crystal Lake just explained how Jason Voorhees survived drowning. Spoilers!

Pam Voorhees was a nightclub singer, married to a man named Elias. To save her pregnancy, she joined his company's secret experiment: Project Argonaut. It gave Jason accelerated healing.

So Jason never died in that lake. He crawled out, and grew up in the woods, while Pam thought he was dead.

In the finale, a grown Jason, with a sack over his head, crushes his father's skull, spares his mother, takes a bullet to the head, and walks away. Pam just laughs. The woods are theirs now.
