Peacock just turned the most famous mother in horror into the star of her own show. And the ending changes everything you thought you knew about Jason Voorhees. Full spoilers ahead. If you haven't finished all eight episodes of Crystal Lake, stop right here.

Crystal Lake is a prequel to the original 1980 Friday the 13th. All eight episodes dropped on Peacock on October 15th. Linda Cardellini plays Pamela Voorhees, a grieving mother whose son Jason drowned at Camp Crystal Lake almost a year before the story begins. The very first scene shows Pam drenched in blood, with a human tooth stuck in her knuckle. So right away, we know: this is not a gentle story about a mom.

Pam isn't just grieving. She's hiding. Around her, you've got Levon Brooks, the local lawman, played by William Catlett. His officer, Dorf, played by Cameron Scoggins. Briana Brooks, who starts digging into what's really going on. Crazy Ralph, the town's prophet of doom. And Grace, a young girl Pam becomes fiercely protective of.

Early on, Pam sees Grace being hurt by her own mother, and soon, something sinister takes revenge on Grace's behalf. As the bodies pile up, the town blames a bear and organizes a hunt. Even so, Camp Crystal Lake reopens for the summer. By episode six, titled "Kill Them, Mommy," a young hippie named Willow has been killed, Briana receives an envelope that cracks her investigation wide open, and Pam finally agrees to tell Levon the truth about her past.

And that past is the big twist. Before Crystal Lake, Pam was a nightclub singer, married to a man named Elias, played by Hamish Linklater. While she was pregnant, she started bleeding heavily during a performance. Desperate to keep her baby, she agreed to an experimental fertility treatment from Elias's company, Bellflower. Its code name: Project Argonaut. The experiments exposed Jason to radiation and drugs, even before he was born.

This is the show's answer to a forty-year-old question: how did Jason survive drowning? Argonaut gave him accelerated healing. Jason never died in that lake. He crawled out, retreated into the woods, and grew up there, while Pam believed her son was dead. The treatment even explains why he grew so big, so fast.

In the finale, Elias and his men arrive in Crystal Lake to take Pam back. Levon refuses to hand her over, a shootout breaks out, and Pam surrenders to stop the bloodshed. Then, he arrives. A grown Jason, with a sack over his head. Elias appeals to Jason as his father. Jason crushes his skull.

Then Jason turns on Pam, and starts to strangle her. But he remembers how she loved him as a child, and he lets her go. Crazy Ralph shoots Jason in the head. It doesn't stop him. Jason escapes into the woods. And Pam? She laughs, proud of how strong her son is, and declares that these woods now belong to her and her boy.

The final shot cuts to teenagers around a campfire, with someone watching from the dark. The show never tells us whether it's Jason, or Pam. Levon's fate is left unclear, and Elias is the only confirmed major death.

Peacock hasn't announced a season two yet. But the pieces are set: a mother hungry for revenge, a son who cannot die, and a camp full of counselors. That's exactly where the 1980 movie begins.
