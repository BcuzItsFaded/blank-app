Another Christmas Carol, but this one comes with a chill.

In Ebenezer, Johnny Depp plays Scrooge, and director Ti West leans hard into the ghost-story side of Dickens. Same setup: a bitter miser, three spirits, one last chance to change.

The big draw is the atmosphere. Shadowy Victorian streets, lavish costumes and eerie imagery make this Christmas feel more haunted than cozy.

But looking spectacular isn't the same as feeling fresh. The story holds few surprises, slow stretches drag, and Depp's performance is divisive, just when the film needs warmth.

The verdict? A beautifully dressed, spooky retelling, but not a new Christmas classic. Come for the ghosts and the Gothic gloom. Just don't expect Scrooge to reinvent Christmas.
