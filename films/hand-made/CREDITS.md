# HAND MADE — credits and licences

All picture elements (matchbox, match, flame spirit, smoke, sparks, starbursts, lettering, 手作 seal) are drawn
procedurally in `main.js` with a custom brush renderer. No fonts are used: the Latin lettering and the two
Chinese characters are brush strokes defined in code. The music and sound effects are synthesised by `audio.py`.
