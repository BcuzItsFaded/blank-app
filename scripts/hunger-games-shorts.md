# The Hunger Games — 5 YouTube Shorts Scripts

PlotTwistWith R&A | 90 seconds each | 10 vertical (9:16) comic panels per video

Upload order (story order): Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes → Sunrise on the Reaping → The Hunger Games → Catching Fire → Mockingjay.

Each panel has a timestamp, narration (voiceover), caption (on-screen text) and an image prompt.

---

## 1. The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes — Ending Explained in 90 Seconds

**Character look (use in every prompt):** Snow — 18, pale, slicked-back blond hair, red-and-white Academy blazer (grey Peacekeeper uniform from Panel 5). Lucy Gray — long dark curly hair, ruffled rainbow dress, acoustic guitar.

**Panel 1 · 0:00–0:09 · HOOK**
- Narration: President Snow was once a broke teenager who fell in love with a girl from District 12. So how did he become a monster?
- Caption: Snow wasn't always evil
- Image prompt: Vertical comic panel. Young Snow in close-up, half his face in shadow. Behind him, older President Snow's silhouette and a white rose. Dramatic red lighting.

**Panel 2 · 0:09–0:18 · SETUP**
- Narration: Ten years after the war, Coriolanus Snow is secretly poor. His family's future depends on one job: mentoring a tribute in the 10th Hunger Games.
- Caption: The 10th Hunger Games
- Image prompt: Snow in his Academy blazer in a grand but crumbling Capitol apartment. Peeling gold wallpaper, an empty dinner plate, cold blue window light.

**Panel 3 · 0:18–0:27 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: He gets the worst draw: Lucy Gray Baird, a singer from District 12. But at the reaping she sings, and the whole Capitol watches.
- Caption: Lucy Gray Baird
- Image prompt: Lucy Gray singing on the reaping stage in her rainbow dress, defiant. Peacekeepers and a stunned crowd behind her, spotlight on her face.

**Panel 4 · 0:27–0:36 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: Snow falls for her. He sneaks her food, plans her strategy, and secretly makes sure the arena's snakes won't attack her. She wins.
- Caption: He cheats for her
- Image prompt: A ruined amphitheater arena, bright-colored snakes pouring across the ground. Lucy Gray stands still, singing, and the snakes curl around her without striking.

**Panel 5 · 0:36–0:45 · TWIST BUILD-UP**
- Narration: But Snow gets caught cheating. As punishment, he's shipped off to District 12 as a Peacekeeper, right back to Lucy Gray.
- Caption: Caught
- Image prompt: Snow in a grey Peacekeeper uniform stepping off a train into a grim coal town. Smokestacks, ash in the air, his blazer left behind on the seat.

**Panel 6 · 0:45–0:54 · TWIST**
- Narration: Here's the twist. His best friend Sejanus plans to help rebels escape. Snow records him and turns him in. Sejanus is hanged.
- Caption: He betrays his best friend
- Image prompt: Snow in the shadows of a dark forest, holding a tracker. Dark-haired Sejanus in the distance, unaware. A mockingjay watches from a branch.

**Panel 7 · 0:54–1:03 · TWIST FALLOUT**
- Narration: Snow has blood on his hands too. On the run with Lucy Gray, he starts to fear she could expose him.
- Caption: Love or survival?
- Image prompt: Snow and Lucy Gray by a lake in the woods at dusk. He grips a rusted rifle while looking at her, and his face turns cold.

**Panel 8 · 1:03–1:12 · ENDING**
- Narration: Lucy Gray senses it. She vanishes into the woods. Snow fires into the trees, and her fate is never revealed.
- Caption: Where did she go?
- Image prompt: A misty forest at night. Lucy Gray's rainbow skirt disappears between the trees while Snow fires blindly into the fog.

**Panel 9 · 1:12–1:21 · ENDING EXPLAINED**
- Narration: Snow returns to the Capitol, secures his family's future, and chooses power over love. The boy is gone. The president has begun.
- Caption: Power over love
- Image prompt: Snow in a white suit on a balcony above a red-bannered Capitol plaza at golden hour. A white rose is pinned to his lapel and his expression is calm and empty.

**Panel 10 · 1:21–1:30 · CLOSING QUESTION**
- Narration: So was Snow always evil, or did the Games make him that way? Tell me in the comments.
- Caption: Born evil or made evil?
- Image prompt: A split panel. Left half is young Snow in his Academy blazer, right half is old President Snow. A single white rose sits on the dividing line.

## 2. Sunrise on the Reaping — Ending Explained in 90 Seconds

**Character look:** Haymitch — 16, messy dark curls, grey eyes, worn work shirt. Lenore Dove — dark wavy hair, flowers tucked in it, a bright homemade dress. Snow — middle-aged, white rose on his lapel.

**Panel 1 · 0:00–0:09 · HOOK**
- Narration: Before Haymitch was a drunk mentor, he was a sixteen-year-old who beat the Capitol at its own game. And Snow made him pay for it.
- Caption: Haymitch's secret past
- Image prompt: A split panel. Left half is grizzled, drunk Haymitch holding a bottle. Right half is young Haymitch staring defiantly at the viewer. Moody amber light.

**Panel 2 · 0:09–0:18 · SETUP**
- Narration: It's the 50th Hunger Games, a Quarter Quell. This year, twice as many kids are reaped. Forty-eight tributes. One winner.
- Caption: 48 tributes. 1 winner.
- Image prompt: The District 12 reaping square, crowded and grey, with "50" on the Capitol screen. Peacekeepers line the edges and frightened kids stand in rows.

**Panel 3 · 0:18–0:27 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: Haymitch is taken from his family and his girlfriend, Lenore Dove, and thrown into the Games.
- Caption: Taken from everyone he loves
- Image prompt: Peacekeepers drag Haymitch away while Lenore Dove reaches for him through the crowd, crying. Rain and cold blue light.

**Panel 4 · 0:27–0:36 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: In the arena, he doesn't just try to survive. He joins a secret plan to sabotage the Games themselves.
- Caption: Breaking the arena
- Image prompt: Haymitch crouching in a too-beautiful arena with candy-colored flowers and poisoned water. He's holding a stolen tool and looking up at the sky.

**Panel 5 · 0:36–0:45 · TWIST BUILD-UP**
- Narration: His allies fall one by one. Soon it's just Haymitch and one last tribute, at the very edge of the arena.
- Caption: The final two
- Image prompt: A cliff edge at sunset. Bloodied Haymitch faces a tribute raising an axe, and the arena's shimmering force field glows behind them.

**Panel 6 · 0:45–0:54 · TWIST**
- Narration: She throws her axe. It flies over the edge, hits the force field, and bounces straight back into her. Haymitch wins by using the arena against the Capitol.
- Caption: The axe comes back
- Image prompt: The axe bouncing off a glowing hexagonal force field in mid-air. Haymitch is in the foreground, wide-eyed, and the sky is red-orange.

**Panel 7 · 0:54–1:03 · TWIST FALLOUT**
- Narration: But Snow saw what he did, and Snow doesn't forgive. Haymitch comes home to find his house burned down. His mother and little brother are dead.
- Caption: Snow's revenge
- Image prompt: Haymitch in a victor's coat standing in front of a burned-out house in District 12. Smoke rises and Snow's face looms on a screen behind him.

**Panel 8 · 1:03–1:12 · ENDING**
- Narration: Then Snow sets up one final cruelty. Haymitch unknowingly gives Lenore Dove a poisoned gumdrop. As she dies in his arms, she makes him promise to stop the Games.
- Caption: Lenore Dove's last wish
- Image prompt: Haymitch kneeling in a meadow, cradling Lenore Dove among scattered wildflowers. A small candy lies in the grass and tears streak his face.

**Panel 9 · 1:12–1:21 · ENDING EXPLAINED**
- Narration: Haymitch drowns his pain in alcohol for years. But when Katniss and Peeta come along, he finally keeps his promise and helps bring Snow down.
- Caption: The promise he kept
- Image prompt: Older Haymitch with a bottle in a dark kitchen. Through the window, two young District 12 tributes are seen in silhouette under a dawn sky.

**Panel 10 · 1:21–1:30 · CLOSING QUESTION**
- Narration: Knowing all this, was Haymitch the true hero of The Hunger Games? Tell me in the comments.
- Caption: The real hero?
- Image prompt: Young and old Haymitch back to back, both looking up at a mockingjay flying across a golden sky.

## 3. The Hunger Games — Ending Explained in 90 Seconds

**Character look:** Katniss — 16, dark side braid, grey eyes, olive skin, hunting jacket, bow. Peeta — 16, curly blond hair, blue eyes, broad shoulders. Prim — 12, blonde braids.

**Panel 1 · 0:00–0:09 · HOOK**
- Narration: One handful of berries started a revolution. Here's how a sixteen-year-old girl beat the Hunger Games.
- Caption: The berries that started a war
- Image prompt: Close-up of a hand holding dark purple nightlock berries, with Katniss's determined eyes in the background. Dramatic rim light.

**Panel 2 · 0:09–0:18 · SETUP**
- Narration: In Panem, every year, twenty-four kids fight to the death on live TV. This year, Katniss Everdeen's little sister Prim gets reaped.
- Caption: Prim is chosen
- Image prompt: The District 12 reaping square. Prim walking forward alone, small and terrified, as Peacekeepers in white armor watch. Grey sky.

**Panel 3 · 0:18–0:27 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: Katniss volunteers to take her place. With her is Peeta Mellark, the baker's son, who tells the whole country he's in love with her.
- Caption: "I volunteer!"
- Image prompt: Katniss pulling Prim into her arms and shielding her, stepping forward with fierce eyes. Peeta stands on the stage behind them.

**Panel 4 · 0:27–0:36 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: In the arena, Katniss hunts, hides, and protects a little girl named Rue. When Rue is killed, Katniss covers her in flowers and defies the cameras.
- Caption: For Rue
- Image prompt: A forest clearing. Katniss kneeling beside small Rue, who is covered in white wildflowers. Katniss raises three fingers to the sky.

**Panel 5 · 0:36–0:45 · TWIST BUILD-UP**
- Narration: Then the Capitol announces a new rule. Two tributes from the same district can win together. Katniss finds Peeta badly wounded and saves his life.
- Caption: Two winners?
- Image prompt: A cave by a stream. Katniss tending to wounded Peeta, firelight on their faces, with a silver parachute beside them.

**Panel 6 · 0:45–0:54 · TWIST**
- Narration: They become the final two. And then the Capitol takes it back. Only one of them can live.
- Caption: The rule is gone
- Image prompt: Katniss and Peeta at the arena's golden Cornucopia at night, looking up in shock at a giant floating announcement. Cold searchlights.

**Panel 7 · 0:54–1:03 · ENDING**
- Narration: So Katniss pulls out poisonous nightlock berries. If they both die, the Capitol gets no winner at all.
- Caption: No winner at all
- Image prompt: Katniss and Peeta facing each other, each holding out a palm full of dark berries. Their faces are inches apart and dawn is breaking behind them.

**Panel 8 · 1:03–1:12 · ENDING**
- Narration: At the last second, the Capitol gives in. Both of them win.
- Caption: "Stop!"
- Image prompt: A huge screen in the Capitol showing the berries frozen at their lips. A stunned Capitol crowd watches, holding their breath.

**Panel 9 · 1:12–1:21 · ENDING EXPLAINED**
- Narration: But Snow is furious. Katniss made the Capitol look weak, and across the districts, people start seeing her as a symbol of rebellion.
- Caption: A symbol is born
- Image prompt: Katniss in a fiery red dress on the victory stage. Behind her, a burning mockingjay emblem, and the cold face of President Snow in the shadows.

**Panel 10 · 1:21–1:30 · CLOSING QUESTION**
- Narration: Was Katniss really in love with Peeta, or was it all a game to survive? Tell me in the comments.
- Caption: Real love or strategy?
- Image prompt: Katniss and Peeta on a train, sitting apart and staring out separate windows. District landscapes blur past.

## 4. Catching Fire — Ending Explained in 90 Seconds

**Character look:** Katniss and Peeta — same look as the first book, a little older; Katniss wears a black arena suit in the arena. Finnick — bronze hair, sea-green eyes, trident. Haymitch — shaggy hair, older. Snow — white beard, white rose.

**Panel 1 · 0:00–0:09 · HOOK**
- Narration: Katniss survived the Hunger Games. So President Snow found a way to throw her back in.
- Caption: Back into the arena
- Image prompt: Katniss in her black arena suit rising up a launch tube into blinding light. Her face is set and her bow is on her back.

**Panel 2 · 0:09–0:18 · SETUP**
- Narration: After her berry stunt, rebellion is spreading. Snow visits Katniss in person with a warning: convince Panem it was all for love, or her family pays.
- Caption: Snow's warning
- Image prompt: Snow sitting in Katniss's study in the Victor's Village. A white rose is on the desk and Katniss stands rigid by the door. Cold, sinister light.

**Panel 3 · 0:18–0:27 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: She fails. So for the 75th Hunger Games, Snow announces the tributes will be chosen from past winners. Katniss is going back in.
- Caption: The Quarter Quell
- Image prompt: Katniss watching the TV announcement in horror, Snow's face filling the screen. Haymitch stands behind her with a bottle.

**Panel 4 · 0:27–0:36 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: Peeta volunteers to go with her. In the arena, they team up with other victors like Finnick and Beetee, while poison fog, killer monkeys and lightning try to kill them.
- Caption: Deadlier than ever
- Image prompt: Katniss, Peeta and Finnick running through a dense tropical jungle as a wall of poison fog rolls toward them. A lightning storm crackles above.

**Panel 5 · 0:36–0:45 · TWIST BUILD-UP**
- Narration: Beetee has a plan: send lightning down a wire and into the arena. Katniss ties the wire to an arrow.
- Caption: The wire
- Image prompt: Katniss on a dark beach at night, wrapping a glowing wire around an arrow. Lightning flashes over the jungle behind her.

**Panel 6 · 0:45–0:54 · TWIST**
- Narration: But she realizes who the real enemy is. She aims at the sky instead and fires the arrow straight into the arena's force field.
- Caption: Remember who the real enemy is
- Image prompt: Katniss firing the electrified arrow straight up. Lightning rides the wire and hits a glowing hexagon dome, which shatters like glass.

**Panel 7 · 0:54–1:03 · TWIST FALLOUT**
- Narration: The arena explodes. Katniss wakes up on a hovercraft and learns the truth. Haymitch, Finnick and the new head Gamemaker, Plutarch, were rebels all along. The plan was to save her.
- Caption: It was a rescue
- Image prompt: Katniss waking up on a hovercraft. Haymitch and a calm, heavyset man with a goatee stand over her, with the sky visible through the window.

**Panel 8 · 1:03–1:12 · ENDING**
- Narration: But they couldn't save everyone. Peeta has been captured by the Capitol.
- Caption: Peeta is gone
- Image prompt: Peeta in chains, being taken by Peacekeepers through a white Capitol corridor. He looks back over his shoulder.

**Panel 9 · 1:12–1:21 · ENDING EXPLAINED**
- Narration: Then her friend Gale delivers the worst news. District 12 has been bombed. It's gone. The war has begun.
- Caption: District 12 is gone
- Image prompt: A burning District 12 seen from above at night. Bombers fly over, and a lone mockingjay escapes through the smoke.

**Panel 10 · 1:21–1:30 · CLOSING QUESTION**
- Narration: Should Haymitch have told Katniss the plan, or was keeping her in the dark the only way? Tell me in the comments.
- Caption: Right to keep the secret?
- Image prompt: Katniss and Haymitch face to face, angry and hurt. A cracked force-field dome is reflected in a window behind them.

## 5. Mockingjay — Ending Explained in 90 Seconds

**Character look:** Katniss — black armored Mockingjay suit, braid, bow. Coin — grey hair cut straight, grey suit. Snow — frail now, white beard. Prim — 13, blonde braids, medic uniform. Peeta — blond, scarred.

**Panel 1 · 0:00–0:09 · HOOK**
- Narration: Katniss won the war. Then she aimed her arrow at the wrong leader. Or maybe the right one.
- Caption: The arrow that ended it all
- Image prompt: Katniss drawing her bow on a stage full of soldiers, her face cold and certain. The arrow is pointed away from the bound prisoner.

**Panel 2 · 0:09–0:18 · SETUP**
- Narration: District 12 is destroyed. The survivors hide in District 13, a secret underground military state led by President Coin.
- Caption: District 13
- Image prompt: A vast grey underground bunker with rows of people in identical grey uniforms. Coin watches from a balcony above.

**Panel 3 · 0:18–0:27 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: Katniss agrees to become the Mockingjay, the face of the rebellion. Her speeches unite the districts.
- Caption: The Mockingjay
- Image prompt: Katniss in a black armored suit in front of burning ruins. A camera crew films her and huge red mockingjay banners rise behind her.

**Panel 4 · 0:27–0:36 · SUMMARY**
- Narration: But the Capitol has brainwashed Peeta to hate her. When he's rescued, he tries to kill her.
- Caption: Peeta turned against her
- Image prompt: A white hospital room. Peeta's hands lunge toward Katniss's throat while guards rush in, his eyes wild and empty.

**Panel 5 · 0:36–0:45 · TWIST BUILD-UP**
- Narration: The rebels storm the Capitol. Outside Snow's mansion, Capitol children wait, and parachutes fall from the sky.
- Caption: The parachutes
- Image prompt: A snowy plaza outside a white mansion. Silver parachutes drift down toward crowds of Capitol children while rebel medics run forward.

**Panel 6 · 0:45–0:54 · TWIST**
- Narration: They explode. Prim, now a medic, is killed right in front of Katniss. And Snow tells her the bombs weren't his. They were Coin's.
- Caption: Prim is gone
- Image prompt: Katniss on her knees screaming, reaching through fire and smoke toward a small blonde figure in a medic uniform. Ash falls like snow.

**Panel 7 · 0:54–1:03 · TWIST FALLOUT**
- Narration: Coin wins and becomes leader. Then she proposes one last Hunger Games using Capitol children. Katniss realizes Coin is just another Snow.
- Caption: A new Snow
- Image prompt: Coin at the head of a long table full of surviving victors, calm and calculating. Katniss stares at her from across the room.

**Panel 8 · 1:03–1:12 · ENDING**
- Narration: At Snow's execution, Katniss raises her bow, turns, and shoots Coin instead. Snow dies in the chaos that follows.
- Caption: She chose Coin
- Image prompt: Katniss releasing an arrow at Coin on a balcony. Snow is tied to a post in the foreground, and the crowd erupts in shock.

**Panel 9 · 1:12–1:21 · ENDING EXPLAINED**
- Narration: The Hunger Games end for good. Katniss returns home, and slowly, she and Peeta heal. Years later, they raise two children in a world without the Games.
- Caption: The cycle is broken
- Image prompt: Katniss and Peeta sitting together in a sunlit meadow full of yellow primroses. Two small children play nearby and green hills glow at sunset.

**Panel 10 · 1:21–1:30 · CLOSING QUESTION**
- Narration: Was Katniss right to kill Coin instead of Snow? Tell me in the comments.
- Caption: Was she right?
- Image prompt: A split panel with Coin's face on the left and Snow's on the right. A single arrow points between them over a red background.
